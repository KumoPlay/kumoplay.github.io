### How to play

Each scene comes with a list of things to find, shown as pictures. Look around, and tap a thing when you spot it. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in, and drag to look around. On a computer, scroll to zoom.

Somewhere in every scene, Kumo is peeking out. Finding Kumo is a secret bonus, with a sticker of its own.

Every scene has three levels: Easy has a few big things, Normal tucks them away a little, and Tricky has tiny things that blend in, sometimes three of the same. The list and the hiding places change every time you play.

### Take your time

There is no timer and no score. A wrong tap just gets a soft wobble. Hints are free and fill up again by themselves. If you stop halfway, the scene waits for you just as you left it.

### Your game stays on your device

Peek-a-Kumo has no accounts and collects no data. Your stickers and scenes are saved in your browser. In Settings you can make a backup code to keep them safe or move them to another phone or computer.

### How to play

Tap an empty plot, pick a seed, and come back when it has grown. Tap a ready crop to harvest it into your barn.

Open **Orders** to see what your sky neighbours would like. Deliver an order to earn coins and stars. Stars raise your level, and new levels bring new seeds, animals, buildings and cloud patches.

Feed your hens and goats and they give eggs and milk. The Cloud Oven and the Creamery turn what you grow into bread, muffins, cheese and more.

### Take your time

Everything simply waits for you. Crops never wilt, animals are never unhappy, and there is nothing to lose by being away.

### Your farm stays on your device

Kumo Farm has no accounts and collects no data. Your farm is saved in your browser. In Settings you can make a backup code to keep it safe or move it to another phone or computer.

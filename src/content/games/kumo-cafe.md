### How to play

Guests sit at the counter and show what they would like. Tap a jar to start cooking, and tap the food when it is ready: it goes to the guest who wants it. Some dishes are put together on a plate, like pancakes with strawberries on top. You can also drag food to the guest or plate of your choice.

Each shift is a few guests, not a countdown. Serve them quickly for bigger tips and up to three stars, then spend your coins on a bigger kitchen and pretty decorations.

### Take your time

Guests never leave angry, food never burns, and every shift ends with at least one star. In Relaxed mode, guests wait as long as you need. After a few shifts, the All-Day Café opens for anyone who wants to keep cooking.

### Your café stays on your device

Kumo Café has no accounts and collects no data. Your progress is saved in your browser. In Settings you can make a backup code to keep it safe or move it to another phone or computer.

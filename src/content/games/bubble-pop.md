### How to play

There are three toys, one tap apart in the bar at the bottom.

**Bubble wrap.** Tap the bubbles to pop them, with as many fingers as you like, or slide a finger along a row. When a sheet is done, a fresh one slides in, with new colors and shapes.

**Soap bubbles.** Press and hold to blow bubbles from a wand. Sweep the wand and the bubbles drift that way. Touch them to pop them, or let them float.

**Squishy cloud.** Poke it and it giggles. Pull it and it stretches, then wobbles back. Pinch it with two fingers and it scrunches up.

### Nothing to win, nothing to lose

There is no timer, no score and no streak. Every so often a surprise turns up: a new bubble-wrap color or pattern, a new wand, or a new face or hat for the cloud. Once in a while, a golden bubble with Kumo inside floats by. Pick your favorites in the collection.

### Calm and quiet

Every pop sounds a little different, and a tiny buzz comes with each pop on phones that can. Soft breeze or wind-chime sounds can play in the background if you like. There is a calm-motion setting, and nothing ever flashes.

### Your toy stays on your device

Bubble Pop has no accounts and collects no data. Your collection and settings are saved in your browser. Add it to your home screen and it works without internet.
